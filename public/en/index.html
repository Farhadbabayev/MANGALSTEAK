Every cut has its own story

Mangal Steak House began in 2014 as a small mangal grill. Today, in the centre of Baku, we bring premium steak and the Azerbaijani mangal tradition to one table. Our meat comes from local farms, our steaks are dry-aged for 28 days in our own ageing room, and our kebabs are cooked only over oak and mulberry charcoal. Because real flavour doesn't like to be rushed.
